# Chapter 6: 基于解释的判断

## Core Idea

人们通过构建解释和故事来判断可能性、责任和因果。故事越连贯，越容易被当作真实，但连贯性不等于概率。

## Frameworks Introduced

- **Story Construction**: evidence is organized into a causal narrative.
- **Scenario Probability Inflation**: detailed scenarios can feel more likely because they are easier to imagine.
- **Hindsight Bias Mechanism**: known outcomes reorganize memory and causal explanation.

## Key Concepts

- **Explanation-based judgment**: judging by explanatory coherence.
- **Narrative coherence**: internal fit among events in a story.
- **Alternative explanation**: a rival causal account of the same facts.

## Mental Models

Use "same evidence, rival stories." If the same facts support multiple stories, confidence should drop until discriminating evidence is found.

## Anti-patterns

- **Single-story certainty**: accepting the first coherent explanation.
- **Causal overfitting**: building a rich causal story from sparse evidence.

## Worked Example

In a legal case, evidence presented in chronological story order may feel more persuasive than the same evidence presented by witness type. A decision aid should separate evidential strength from narrative ease.

## Key Takeaways

1. Story coherence can dominate probability.
2. Always construct rival explanations.
3. Hindsight makes outcomes feel more predictable than they were.
