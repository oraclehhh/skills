# Chapter 14: 赞美不确定性

## Core Idea

不确定性不是理性的敌人，而是理性发挥作用的场域。好的判断不是消灭不确定性，而是承认它、表达它、校准它，并在其中做出稳健选择。

## Frameworks Introduced

- **Calibrated Humility**: state confidence in proportion to evidence.
- **Robust Decision Making**: prefer options that remain acceptable under plausible errors.
- **Uncertainty Appreciation**: use uncertainty to motivate learning, feedback, and reversible commitments.

## Key Concepts

- **Calibration**: match confidence to actual accuracy.
- **Robustness**: performing well across multiple possible states.
- **Reversibility**: ability to change course after new information.

## Mental Models

Use "confidence is a variable, not a decoration." Every recommendation should expose what is known, unknown, assumed, and decision-relevant.

## Anti-patterns

- **False certainty**: hiding uncertainty to sound decisive.
- **Paralysis by analysis**: using uncertainty as a reason never to choose.

## Worked Example

For a strategic choice with uncertain market demand, a robust decision may be a staged launch: small commitment, feedback collection, and expansion only if evidence improves. This respects uncertainty without freezing action.

## Key Takeaways

1. Good decisions make uncertainty visible.
2. Calibration beats overconfidence.
3. Robust, reversible choices are often wiser than fragile optimal plans.
