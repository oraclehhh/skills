# Chapter 11: 理性决策理论

## Core Idea

理性决策理论提供规范框架：列出行动、概率和效用，选择期望效用最高且对关键假设稳健的方案。

## Frameworks Introduced

- **Expected Utility Maximization**: choose the option with the highest probability-weighted utility.
- **Decision Tree Analysis**: model action branches, chance branches, and terminal values.
- **Sensitivity Analysis**: test whether the choice changes under plausible probability or utility shifts.
- **Decision Threshold**: choose action when expected benefit exceeds expected cost.

## Key Concepts

- **Expected value**: probability-weighted average monetary outcome.
- **Expected utility**: probability-weighted average subjective value.
- **Risk attitude**: preference pattern over uncertain outcomes.
- **Threshold**: point where action becomes worthwhile.

## Mental Models

Use "make the assumptions negotiable." The value of decision analysis is often not the final number but the visible structure of disagreement.

## Anti-patterns

- **False precision**: treating guessed probabilities as authoritative.
- **No sensitivity check**: making a recommendation without knowing which assumption drives it.

## Worked Example

A company deciding whether to launch can model success, moderate adoption, and failure. Assign probabilities and utilities to each branch. Then ask what probability of success would be required for launch to beat delay or cancellation.

## Key Takeaways

1. Rational theory is a benchmark for disciplined choice.
2. Decision trees expose assumptions.
3. Sensitivity analysis is mandatory when estimates are uncertain.
