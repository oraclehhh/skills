# Chapter 2: 何谓决策

## Core Idea

决策是从备选项中选择行动的过程，它包含问题表征、备选项生成、结果预测、价值评价和选择程序。许多错误来自把过去成本、情绪或默认路径误当作当前选择的理由。

## Frameworks Introduced

- **Decision as Procedure**: define the steps that transform uncertainty and value into action.
- **Decision Tree Representation**: represent actions, chance events, and outcomes in a tree to make assumptions visible.
- **Sunk-Cost Principle**: unrecoverable past costs should not drive future choice.

## Key Concepts

- **Alternative**: a feasible action path.
- **Consequence**: what may happen after choosing an alternative.
- **Sunk cost**: already-paid cost that cannot be recovered.
- **Opportunity cost**: value of the best foregone option.

## Mental Models

Use "start today" reasoning: if you had not already invested, would you choose this option now?

## Anti-patterns

- **Continuing because of past investment**: treating effort already spent as a reason to spend more.
- **Undefined alternatives**: deciding between a named option and vague dissatisfaction.

## Worked Example

A team has spent months on a failing project. The sunk-cost reset asks them to ignore the spent months and compare only future effort, likely payoff, salvage value, and alternative uses of the team. This reframes "waste the past" into "choose the best future."

## Key Takeaways

1. A decision must include alternatives, uncertainty, and value.
2. Decision trees prevent hidden assumptions.
3. Past unrecoverable costs are psychologically powerful but normatively irrelevant.
