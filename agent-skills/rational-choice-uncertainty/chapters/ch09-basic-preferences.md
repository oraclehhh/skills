# Chapter 9: 对后果的评价：基本偏好

## Core Idea

决策不仅取决于事件概率，还取决于人如何评价后果。效用、情绪、幸福、适应和参照点都会改变结果的主观价值。

## Frameworks Introduced

- **Utility Evaluation**: value outcomes by subjective usefulness or desirability, not only objective amount.
- **Hedonic Adaptation**: affective reactions often shift after people adapt to circumstances.
- **Reference-Dependent Evaluation**: outcomes are experienced relative to expectations or comparison points.

## Key Concepts

- **Utility**: subjective value of an outcome.
- **Preference**: comparative evaluation among outcomes.
- **Adaptation**: emotional response changes as a condition becomes normal.
- **Reference point**: baseline used to interpret gains and losses.

## Mental Models

Use "value is not money." Ask how each outcome changes goals, emotions, identity, social position, and future options.

## Anti-patterns

- **Objective-value substitution**: assuming the largest monetary result has the greatest utility.
- **Ignoring adaptation**: overestimating the lasting emotional impact of many outcomes.

## Worked Example

Two jobs may differ in salary, autonomy, status, commute, and learning. A utility analysis requires weighting consequences that the person will actually experience, including how quickly the initial excitement or disappointment will adapt.

## Key Takeaways

1. Decision analysis needs utilities, not only outcomes.
2. Preferences are influenced by reference points and adaptation.
3. A good choice model asks which consequences matter to the chooser.
