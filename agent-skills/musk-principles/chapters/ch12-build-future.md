# Chapter 12: 建设我们的未来

## Core Idea
公司可以被看作建设未来的工具。值得创业的领域通常不仅赚钱，还能推动文明能力、改善生活并创造广泛财富。

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Company as Charity at Scale**: a company can serve humanity by solving real problems sustainably.
- **Shared Wealth Creation**: durable companies create value for customers, workers, investors, and society.
- **Civilization-Advancing Fields**: choose domains that unlock future capability.

## Key Concepts
- **Civilizational leverage**: work that changes what society can do.
- **Entrepreneurial field**: domain worthy of company creation.
- **Value distribution**: who benefits from company success.

## Mental Models
Use "future infrastructure filter": does this company build something future generations will rely on?

## Anti-patterns
- **Thin opportunity chasing**: building for arbitrage rather than durable usefulness.
- **Zero-sum entrepreneurship**: extracting value without expanding capability.

## Worked Example
A startup in energy storage, reusable launch, or autonomy can be evaluated by whether it reduces a bottleneck for many downstream activities.

## Key Takeaways
1. Companies can be tools for large-scale progress.
2. Good businesses create broad value.
3. Future-building domains deserve disproportionate attention.
