# Chapter 8: 以制造为本

## Core Idea
制造不是产品之后的环节，制造就是产品的一部分。真正的护城河常在工厂、流程、供应链和最薄弱环节的持续改进中。

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Factory as Product**: design the production system as carefully as the product.
- **Weakest-Link Attack**: improve the constraint that limits throughput or quality.
- **Hands-On Manufacturing**: do real work, inspect physical processes, and learn from production.
- **Manufacturing Moat**: scale, quality, and cost can be deeper advantages than concept.

## Key Concepts
- **Throughput**: production output over time.
- **Bottleneck**: slowest or most fragile step.
- **Process design**: arrangement of tools, people, and flow.

## Mental Models
Use "prototype is chapter one." A product is not mature until the factory can make it repeatedly at target cost and quality.

## Anti-patterns
- **Prototype triumphalism**: celebrating a demo before production is solved.
- **Automation-first error**: automating a bad or unnecessary process.

## Worked Example
If one assembly step limits output, redesign that station, part, or sequence before adding more workers or machines elsewhere.

## Key Takeaways
1. Manufacturing is strategic.
2. Attack the bottleneck, not the most visible problem.
3. Automate only after deletion and simplification.
