# Chapter 2: 像物理学家一样思考

## Core Idea
不要从行业惯例出发，要从真理、约束和物理极限出发。第一性原理思维把“不可能”拆成可检验的材料、成本、能量、时间和设计问题。

## Frameworks Introduced
- **First-Principles Thinking**: reason from fundamentals rather than analogy.
- **Limit Thinking**: locate the real theoretical boundary.
- **Error Reduction**: assume you are wrong somewhere; design learning loops to find it.

## Key Concepts
- **Assumption**: a belief inherited from convention.
- **Physical constraint**: a real limit from physics or engineering.
- **Analogy reasoning**: copying prior practice without rebuilding the logic.

## Mental Models
Use "what must be true?" before asking "what do others do?"

## Anti-patterns
- **Industry fatalism**: accepting current cost or speed as natural law.
- **Complexity worship**: treating hard-to-understand systems as impossible to change.

## Worked Example
If rockets seem inherently expensive, break the cost into materials, manufacturing, reuse, launch operations, and failure rates. The price charged by incumbents is not the same as the physical cost floor.

## Key Takeaways
1. First principles reveal hidden slack.
2. True constraints differ from inherited practices.
3. Deep understanding turns mysterious problems into ordinary work.
