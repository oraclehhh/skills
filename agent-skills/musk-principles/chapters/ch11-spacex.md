# Chapter 11: 创建 SpaceX

## Core Idea
SpaceX 展示了用第一性原理挑战高成本航天业：理解火箭、承受失败、争取关键合同、让系统先运转起来，再逼近更高极限。

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Rocket First Principles**: compare market prices with material and manufacturing fundamentals.
- **Failure Budgeting**: expect severe setbacks in hard engineering.
- **Operational Survival**: get the system working before optimizing every ideal.
- **Mars-Back Planning**: use the Mars objective to guide rocket architecture.

## Key Concepts
- **Launch cost**: total cost to put payload into orbit.
- **Reuse**: recovering and flying hardware again.
- **Contract validation**: customer commitment that supports survival and scale.

## Mental Models
Use "from Mars backward." If the long-term goal is settlement, work backward to launch cost, reusability, payload, cadence, and manufacturing.

## Anti-patterns
- **Single-shot perfection**: waiting for a perfect system instead of learning from flights.
- **Supplier captivity**: accepting aerospace cost structures without rebuilding them.

## Worked Example
Rather than buying conventional rockets at accepted prices, analyze materials, engines, manufacturing, launch operations, and reuse to find where cost can fall.

## Key Takeaways
1. First principles can reset industry cost assumptions.
2. Survival requires both technical progress and external validation.
3. Mars is used as an organizing constraint, not only a dream.
