# Chapter 9: 成为创始人

## Core Idea
创始人承担极端不确定性，把资源押到少数关键机会，并通过倾听、调整、联合和持续行动提高生存概率。

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Founder Ownership**: take personal responsibility for company survival.
- **All-In Commitment**: concentrate resources when conviction and opportunity align.
- **Listen and Adjust**: combine stubborn mission with flexible tactics.
- **Strong Alliances**: partner where combined capability creates leverage.

## Key Concepts
- **Startup risk**: existential uncertainty in early company building.
- **Iteration**: rapid adjustment from feedback.
- **Commitment**: willingness to endure downside for mission upside.

## Mental Models
Use "mission fixed, path flexible." Do not confuse changing tactics with abandoning the goal.

## Anti-patterns
- **Founder ego lock-in**: refusing feedback because the original plan was yours.
- **Half-commitment**: taking startup risk without enough focus or resource concentration.

## Worked Example
After a first venture exit, reinvesting proceeds into a harder mission can compound capability, but only if paired with learning and strong partners.

## Key Takeaways
1. Founders absorb uncertainty.
2. Listening and speed are survival tools.
3. Concentrated commitment can create leverage and risk.
