# Chapter 15: 成为多行星物种

## Core Idea
成为多行星物种被呈现为人类进化和生命保护的长期里程碑。火星不是逃避地球，而是扩大生命延续和探索的可能性。

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Life Preservation Mission**: protect life by reducing single-planet fragility.
- **Mars Gateway**: build the transport and settlement capabilities needed for another world.
- **New World Construction**: settlement requires energy, habitats, manufacturing, governance, and culture.
- **Galactic Horizon**: use distant ambition to organize present engineering.

## Key Concepts
- **Multi-planetary species**: humans able to sustain civilization beyond Earth.
- **Settlement architecture**: systems needed for life and industry off-world.
- **Planetary resilience**: reduced dependence on one habitat.

## Mental Models
Use "backup and expansion." Space settlement is both insurance for life and an expansion of human possibility.

## Anti-patterns
- **Escape fantasy**: using Mars to ignore Earth problems.
- **Vision without logistics**: imagining settlement without transport, energy, production, and survival systems.

## Worked Example
A Mars plan must work backward from settlement needs: launch cadence, reusable rockets, cargo mass, fuel production, habitats, local resources, and self-sustaining industry.

## Key Takeaways
1. Multi-planetary ambition is a long-term survival strategy.
2. Protecting Earth and expanding beyond it can be complementary.
3. Grand visions must be decomposed into engineering systems.
