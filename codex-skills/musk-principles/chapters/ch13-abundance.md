# Chapter 13: 丰裕时代

## Core Idea
技术进步可以把稀缺转化为丰裕。算力、脑机接口、自动驾驶和可持续能源等领域可能显著提升人类能力和生活质量。

## Frameworks Introduced
- **End of Scarcity Lens**: ask which scarce resource technology can make abundant.
- **Compute Compounding**: increasing computation changes what systems can do.
- **Human Capability Upgrade**: tools can expand cognition, mobility, and agency.
- **Sustainable Abundance**: abundance must be compatible with energy and environmental constraints.

## Key Concepts
- **Scarcity**: limited access to a needed resource.
- **Exponential improvement**: compounding technical progress.
- **Autonomy**: systems performing tasks with less human control.

## Mental Models
Use "what gets cheaper by 10x?" When a key input collapses in cost, new markets and behaviors appear.

## Anti-patterns
- **Static scarcity thinking**: assuming today's limits are permanent.
- **Abundance without sustainability**: increasing output while ignoring energy and environmental costs.

## Worked Example
If autonomous driving becomes reliable and cheap, transportation capacity, safety, urban design, logistics, and time use can all change together.

## Key Takeaways
1. Technology can expand the feasible set.
2. Compute and autonomy are compounding levers.
3. Abundance must be designed sustainably.
