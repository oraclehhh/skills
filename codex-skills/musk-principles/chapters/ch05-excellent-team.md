# Chapter 5: 打造卓越团队

## Core Idea
卓越团队围绕同一目标作战，重视建设者文化、顶尖人才、精兵强将和直接反馈。情面不能高于真相。

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Builder Culture**: reward people who make, test, and solve.
- **Talent Density**: prefer fewer excellent people over many mediocre contributors.
- **Feedback Over Politeness**: make truth faster than comfort.
- **Shared Mission Alignment**: keep everyone oriented toward the same hard goal.

## Key Concepts
- **Builder**: person who creates real output.
- **Talent density**: concentration of high-performing contributors.
- **Direct feedback**: clear signal about what works and what fails.

## Mental Models
Use "team as rocket stage": every added component must justify its mass.

## Anti-patterns
- **Comfort culture**: avoiding hard truth to preserve feelings.
- **Headcount as progress**: treating a larger team as automatically stronger.

## Worked Example
A team missing deadlines should identify the few people who unblock core work, reduce coordination load, and make feedback immediate and specific.

## Key Takeaways
1. Shared mission reduces coordination waste.
2. High standards require direct feedback.
3. Talent density beats organizational mass.
