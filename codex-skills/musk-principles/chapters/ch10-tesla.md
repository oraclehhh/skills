# Chapter 10: 创建特斯拉

## Core Idea
特斯拉被呈现为使命、技术、资本、产品战略和生存压力交织的案例：用高性能产品推动可持续交通，并逐步降低成本、扩大市场。

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Mission-Led Company**: protect the planet by making sustainable transport desirable.
- **Top-Down Market Entry**: start with premium performance, then move toward broader affordability.
- **Survival Execution**: during crisis, prioritize what keeps the mission alive.
- **Narrative War**: public perception can affect capital, demand, and legitimacy.

## Key Concepts
- **Prototype**: initial proof of capability.
- **Scale strategy**: path from niche product to mass market.
- **Public narrative**: external story shaping trust and adoption.

## Mental Models
Use "performance first, scale next" when a new technology must overcome skepticism.

## Anti-patterns
- **Mission without product excellence**: expecting customers to buy virtue alone.
- **Ignoring narrative**: letting critics define a category before the product proves itself.

## Worked Example
An electric vehicle strategy can begin with a high-performance car that changes perception, then use learning and capital to build cheaper, higher-volume models.

## Key Takeaways
1. Mission needs a product strategy.
2. Survival periods require ruthless focus.
3. Public trust and technical execution reinforce each other.
