# Chapter 14: 我们的生存风险

## Core Idea
乐观必须搭配风险意识。战争、过度监管、不可再生能源、失控 AI、人口崩溃和小行星等风险都应被看作文明设计问题。

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Civilizational Risk Register**: explicitly track threats to human flourishing.
- **AI Safety Concern**: powerful intelligence systems require alignment and governance.
- **Energy Transition Imperative**: nonrenewable energy creates long-term fragility.
- **Regulation Balance**: too little governance can be dangerous; too much can freeze progress.

## Key Concepts
- **Existential risk**: threat to long-term survival or flourishing.
- **Alignment**: ensuring AI systems pursue compatible goals.
- **Population collapse**: demographic decline that weakens civilization.

## Mental Models
Use "risk as requirement." If a risk could end the mission, design around it instead of treating it as external.

## Anti-patterns
- **Naive optimism**: assuming progress automatically solves every threat.
- **Paralyzing pessimism**: focusing on danger without building solutions.

## Worked Example
AI capability progress should be paired with safety work, governance, and technical alignment rather than treated as only a race for performance.

## Key Takeaways
1. Long-term optimism requires serious risk management.
2. Some risks are civilization-level design constraints.
3. Progress and safety must be pursued together.

## Connects To

- **Ch 15**: planetary resilience addresses some existential risks.
