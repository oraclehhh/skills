# Glossary

**Abundance**: the view that technology can reduce scarcity and expand access to energy, compute, transport, and capability (Ch 13).

**Builder culture**: a team norm that rewards making, testing, owning, and shipping over status or politics (Ch 5).

**Factory as product**: the production system is itself a central product to design, improve, and scale (Ch 8).

**First-principles thinking**: reasoning from fundamental constraints rather than analogy or convention (Ch 2).

**Founder mode**: direct, high-ownership operating style suited to creating or rescuing companies (Ch 9).

**Limit thinking**: asking where the real physical or economic boundary is, rather than accepting current practice (Ch 2).

**Manufacturing moat**: durable advantage created by superior production systems, not only product design (Ch 8).

**Mission**: a purpose strong enough to determine priorities, sacrifices, and standards (Ch 1, Ch 10).

**Multi-planetary species**: the ambition to expand human presence beyond Earth to improve long-term survival and possibility (Ch 15).

**Parallel execution**: running multiple work streams at once to compress time and expose constraints (Ch 7).

**Useful work**: activity that contributes more than it consumes and creates real-world benefit (Ch 1).

**Work algorithm**: question requirements, delete, simplify, accelerate, then automate (Ch 7, Ch 8).
