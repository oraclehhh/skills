# Chapter 12: 描述性决策理论

## Core Idea

描述性决策理论解释人们实际如何偏离规范模型。偏离不是随机噪声，而是由框架、损失、参照点、后悔和心理账户等因素系统性地产生。

## Frameworks Introduced

- **Prospect Theory**: people evaluate gains and losses relative to a reference point and overweight losses.
- **Loss Aversion**: losses often carry more psychological weight than equal gains.
- **Framing Effect**: equivalent options can trigger different choices when described differently.
- **Sunk-Cost and Escalation**: prior investment increases commitment even when future value is poor.

## Key Concepts

- **Reference point**: baseline defining gain or loss.
- **Value function**: subjective response to gains and losses.
- **Endowment effect**: owning something can increase its perceived value.
- **Mental accounting**: separating money or outcomes into psychological categories.

## Mental Models

Use "what frame am I in?" A choice may look like risk preference, but the real driver may be whether the person sees the situation as protecting gains or avoiding losses.

## Anti-patterns

- **Loss-frame panic**: taking excessive risk to avoid realizing a loss.
- **Ownership inflation**: overvaluing an option because it is already yours.

## Worked Example

A manager may reject a risky but positive expected-value project when framed as possible loss, yet accept equivalent risk when framed as opportunity. Reframing both versions around final wealth and strategic goals can reveal the inconsistency.

## Key Takeaways

1. Human choice is reference-dependent.
2. Losses, frames, and ownership change preferences.
3. Descriptive models predict when rational analysis will be resisted.
