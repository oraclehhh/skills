# Chapter 3: 判断的整体框架

## Core Idea

判断可以看作从线索推断目标。好判断依赖于使用真正有效的线索，并以稳定、可校准的方式整合它们。

## Frameworks Introduced

- **Lens Model**: separates the environment's cue-validity structure from the judge's cue-utilization pattern.
- **Linear Model Baseline**: weighted additive models often perform surprisingly well because they are consistent.
- **Cue-Weight Audit**: compare actual diagnosticity with perceived importance.

## Key Concepts

- **Cue**: an observable signal used for judgment.
- **Criterion**: the target being predicted or evaluated.
- **Ecological validity**: actual predictive relation between cue and criterion.
- **Cue utilization**: the weight a person gives a cue.

## Mental Models

Use "two weight vectors": one vector describes the world; the other describes the judge. Error appears when the judge's weights diverge from the world's weights.

## Anti-patterns

- **Salience equals validity**: overusing vivid, available, or socially prominent cues.
- **Inconsistent expert judgment**: changing cue weights case by case without a valid reason.

## Worked Example

In hiring, interview charisma may receive high cue utilization while work-sample performance may have higher ecological validity. A structured scorecard can shift weight toward more diagnostic cues and reduce noise.

## Key Takeaways

1. Ask which cues actually predict the criterion.
2. Simple linear models are strong baselines for human judgment.
3. Consistency is a major advantage of formal models.
