# Chapter 13: 下一个是什么？判断与决策研究的新方向

## Core Idea

判断与决策研究正在从孤立个体的实验任务扩展到情绪、社会互动、文化、生态环境和真实组织场景。

## Frameworks Introduced

- **Ecological Rationality**: evaluate a strategy by how well it fits the environment.
- **Affect in Decision Making**: emotions can provide information and also distort evaluation.
- **Social Decision Context**: decisions are shaped by groups, norms, accountability, and interaction.

## Key Concepts

- **Ecology**: the environment in which a decision strategy operates.
- **Affect**: emotional response that influences judgment.
- **Accountability**: expectation of explaining or defending a choice.

## Mental Models

Use "fit the tool to the environment." A heuristic that fails in one environment may work well in another if it exploits reliable structure.

## Anti-patterns

- **Laboratory-only confidence**: assuming a finding transfers without checking real-world constraints.
- **Emotion exclusion**: treating affect as only noise rather than possible information.

## Worked Example

In emergency response, a simple recognition-based heuristic may outperform slow calculation when time is short and experts have valid experience. In novel environments, the same intuition may fail and need formal analysis.

## Key Takeaways

1. Decision research must consider environment and context.
2. Emotion and social structure are part of decision processes.
3. Good decision aids fit the setting, not only the theory.
