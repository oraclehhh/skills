# Glossary

**本体**: the original moral clarity of the heart-mind; not an excuse to skip practice.

**诚意**: making intention truthful and free of self-deception.

**传习录**: collection of Wang Yangming's conversations, letters, and teachings compiled by disciples.

**大学**: classic frequently reinterpreted in the text, especially around 明明德, 亲民, 止至善, 格物, 致知, 诚意.

**格物**: rectifying the affair toward which intention is directed; removing what is not upright.

**功夫**: concrete practice of moral cultivation.

**良知**: innate moral knowing that discerns right and wrong.

**明德**: luminous virtue or moral clarity to be made manifest.

**亲民**: Yangming's preferred reading against merely external "newing the people"; emphasizes intimate moral relation.

**去私欲，存天理**: removing selfish desire so moral principle can be present.

**事上磨炼**: cultivating the heart through real affairs rather than escaping them.

**四句教**: late teaching formula linking heart, intention, moral knowing, and rectification.

**天理**: moral principle, not external to the heart.

**心即理**: the heart-mind is principle; there is no moral principle outside the heart.

**意**: intention or direction of the heart's activity.

**知行合一**: genuine knowing and action are one; non-action reveals incomplete or obstructed knowing.

**致良知**: extending and enacting innate moral knowing.

**朱子学**: Cheng-Zhu learning, frequently debated in the text as a contrastive frame.
