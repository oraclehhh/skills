# Chapter 3: 传习录上

## Core Idea
上卷 introduces the most decisive doctrinal pivots: 心即理, 知行合一, 格物 as rectifying intention, and 良知 as innate moral knowing.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **心即理**: moral principle is not outside the heart-mind.
- **知行合一**: genuine knowing is already actional; failure to act indicates obstructed knowing.
- **格物 as 正意**: 物 is the affair where intention rests; 格 is rectification.
- **良知不假外求**: moral knowing is not imported from external investigation.

## Key Concepts
- **徐爱**: early disciple whose questions frame many core issues.
- **亲民**: read as moral intimacy rather than merely textual correction to 新民.
- **至善**: not an external object but the heart's rightly realized condition.
- **私欲**: selfish obstruction that blocks 天理.

## Mental Models
Use "affair where intention lands." If 意 is directed to serving parents, governing, studying, speaking, or desiring, that is the 物 in which 格物 must occur.

## Anti-patterns
- **Externalized principle**: searching for morality only in objects, books, or rules.
- **Verbal knowing**: claiming knowledge that never enters action.
- **Textual victory**: winning an interpretation while losing the practice.

## Worked Example
If someone says they know honesty matters but hides a mistake, Yangming's frame would not say "knowledge exists, action failed." It would say the knowledge is not yet genuine in this affair because fear and self-protection obstruct 良知.

## Key Takeaways
1. 心即理 redirects learning inward without abandoning affairs.
2. 知行合一 turns moral knowledge into a test of conduct.
3. 格物 means rectifying the intention active in concrete affairs.

## Connects To
- **Ch 05**: letters defend these interpretations against critics.
- **Ch 06**: late teachings condense them into 致良知 and 四句教.
