# Chapter 7: 训蒙大意与教约

## Core Idea
教育不是填塞知识，而是养成心志、礼节、习惯和向善的能力。童蒙教育尤其要顺性情、立规范、引导实践。

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Education as Moral Formation**: learning shapes intention and conduct.
- **Habit Before Display**: form daily behavior rather than chase clever performance.
- **Appropriate Guidance**: teach according to age, temperament, and stage.
- **Discipline With Purpose**: rules should help preserve moral clarity, not become empty control.

## Key Concepts
- **训蒙**: instruction of the young.
- **教约**: educational rules or compact.
- **礼**: embodied form that trains attention and relation.
- **志**: direction of the learner's will.

## Mental Models
Use "education as cultivating direction." Ask not only what the learner remembers, but what they love, avoid, repeat, and enact.

## Anti-patterns
- **Rote-only learning**: memory without moral transformation.
- **Harsh control**: discipline that breaks vitality rather than forms character.
- **Cleverness worship**: rewarding verbal performance over conduct.

## Worked Example
A student who can recite moral texts but lies under pressure needs practice in honesty, accountability, and reflection more than another passage to memorize.

## Key Takeaways
1. Education forms intention.
2. Ritual and habit can support moral clarity.
3. Good teaching returns knowledge to conduct.
