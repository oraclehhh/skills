# Chapter 8: 附录《朱子晚年定论》

## Core Idea
The appendix positions Yangming's teaching in relation to Zhu Xi by emphasizing places where Zhu Xi's later views are read as closer to inward realization and practical cultivation than rigid externalized learning.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Doctrinal Reconciliation Attempt**: read opponents at their strongest and most mature.
- **Against Fixed Labels**: do not reduce Zhu Xi or Yangming to slogans.
- **Return to Practice**: judge doctrines by whether they restore moral effort to the heart and affairs.

## Key Concepts
- **朱子晚年定论**: selected late Zhu Xi statements interpreted as converging with Yangming concerns.
- **程朱理学**: major intellectual background for the debates.
- **阳明学定位**: locating Yangming in continuity and critique.

## Mental Models
Use "strong reading before critique." Before rejecting a school, ask whether its mature form contains a corrective to the caricature.

## Anti-patterns
- **Sectarian reading**: defending a school identity rather than truth.
- **Caricature critique**: attacking simplified Zhu Xi or simplified Yangming.
- **Historical flattening**: ignoring development across a thinker's life.

## Worked Example
When comparing Zhu Xi and Yangming on 格物, do not only contrast "external things" with "inner heart." Ask how each view tries to secure moral transformation, and where each can become distorted in practice.

## Key Takeaways
1. Yangming learning defines itself through both continuity and critique.
2. Mature doctrines should be read generously.
3. The final test is whether learning transforms intention and action.
