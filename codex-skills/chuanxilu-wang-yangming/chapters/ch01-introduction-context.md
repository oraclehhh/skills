# Chapter 1: 前言与成书脉络

## Core Idea
《传习录》不是系统论文，而是师友问答、书信、门人记录汇集而成。理解它要抓住阳明心学的核心问题：如何从人的本心出发重建道德实践。

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Text-as-Practice Record**: read the book as teaching situations, not only propositions.
- **Heart-Mind Center**: 心 is the center for interpreting 心即理, 知行合一, 致良知.
- **Historical Formation**: 上、中、下卷来自不同记录和编纂阶段.

## Key Concepts
- **王阳明**: 明代思想家、政治家、军事家，阳明心学核心人物.
- **龙场悟道**: discovering that the way of the sage is sufficient in one's own nature.
- **门人记录**: disciples preserve teachings through questions and conversations.

## Mental Models
Use "occasioned teaching": many statements answer a particular student's obstruction. Do not flatten every line into abstract doctrine.

## Anti-patterns
- **Quote collecting**: extracting aphorisms without the question they answer.
- **Doctrine isolation**: treating 心即理 or 知行合一 apart from cultivation practice.

## Worked Example
When reading a passage on 格物, first ask who is asking, what confusion they bring from Zhu Xi learning, and how Yangming redirects them toward intention and practice.

## Key Takeaways
1. The text is practical pedagogy.
2. 心学 concepts mutually define one another.
3. Edition history matters, but application requires returning to practice.
