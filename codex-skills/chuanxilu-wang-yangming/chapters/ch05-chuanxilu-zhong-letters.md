# Chapter 5: 传习录中：论学书信

## Core Idea
中卷书信 develops and defends Yangming's doctrine in dialogue with critics, especially around 《大学》, Zhu Xi learning, 致良知, and the danger of mistaking external investigation for moral cultivation.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **致良知 as Core Work**: extend innate moral knowing into every affair.
- **Against Externalized 格物**: do not scatter the mind among objects while neglecting intention.
- **拔本塞源 Diagnosis**: doctrines can sever the root if they make principle external and practice derivative.
- **Letter-as-Correction**: written responses tailor doctrine to particular misunderstandings.

## Key Concepts
- **答顾东桥书**: major doctrinal defense.
- **答陆原静书**: clarifies learning and practice.
- **答罗整庵少宰书**: engages criticism of Yangming learning.
- **朱子**: both honored and debated as a contrastive authority.

## Mental Models
Use "where is the root?" When a doctrine produces scattered effort, pride in knowledge, or delay of action, ask whether it has cut practice away from the heart.

## Anti-patterns
- **Scholastic displacement**: discussing doctrine to avoid rectifying intention.
- **Authority hiding**: citing old masters to avoid one's own moral seeing.
- **Separated learning**: treating knowledge, sincerity, and action as distant stages.

## Worked Example
A manager reads ethics books but keeps manipulating employees. A Yangming response would not begin with more ethical theory; it would ask what the manager already knows, what desire blocks it, and where to enact correction.

## Key Takeaways
1. 致良知 is the central mature formula.
2. Critique of Zhu Xi learning targets externalization, not learning itself.
3. Letters show doctrine under pressure.
