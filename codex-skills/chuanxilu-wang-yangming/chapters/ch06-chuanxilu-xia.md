# Chapter 6: 传习录下

## Core Idea
下卷 gathers later teachings where 致良知 becomes the central thread. The work is direct, concrete, and severe: recognize 良知, remove selfish obstruction, and act.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **致良知**: extend moral knowing until it governs affairs.
- **四句教**: map heart, intention, moral discernment, and rectification.
- **For-Good Remove-Evil**: practice is not merely insight; it is moral transformation in conduct.
- **No Separate Sacred Space**: ordinary affairs are enough for full cultivation.

## Key Concepts
- **天泉证道**: late teaching context associated with the 四句教.
- **心之体**: original heart condition.
- **意之动**: movement of intention where good and evil appear.
- **为善去恶**: doing good and removing evil as practical 格物.

## Mental Models
Use "four layers of diagnosis": original heart, moving intention, moral knowing, corrective action. Locate which layer the current problem confuses.

## Anti-patterns
- **Using 无善无恶 to excuse conduct**: mistaking ontological language for moral permissiveness.
- **Knowing without removal**: discerning good and evil but keeping the obstruction.
- **Seeking special practice**: ignoring ordinary duties while looking for spiritual technique.

## Worked Example
In resentment toward a colleague, the original heart is not the resentment. The intention has moved with partiality. 良知 knows the unfairness. 格物 means removing that crookedness in speech and action.

## Key Takeaways
1. 致良知 is active moral extension.
2. 四句教 is a diagnostic map.
3. Practice must appear in ordinary affairs.
